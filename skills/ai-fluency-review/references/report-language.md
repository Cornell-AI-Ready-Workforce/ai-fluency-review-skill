# Report language

Use these labels exactly in the participant-facing HTML.

## Areas

| Research area | Action label | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Description | Brief the task | Snapshot and Area details |
| Delegation | Divide the work | Snapshot and Area details |
| Discernment | Check the result | Snapshot and Area details |
| Diligence | Finish responsibly | Snapshot and Area details |
| Adaptive Flexibility | Learn and adapt | Snapshot and How your approach changed |

Do not add Adaptive Flexibility to Area details.

## Ratings

1. Rarely observed
2. Emerging
3. Usually observed
4. Consistent across contexts
5. Consistent and reusable
6. Not enough evidence (`null` only)

These are behavior descriptions, not scores, grades, credentials, or judgments of the person.

## Evidence language

- **What we observed:** concise account of behavior supported by the trace.
- **Where it was inconsistent:** observed counterexample or condition where the pattern weakened. If none was observed, say so without claiming none exists.
- **What we could not observe:** unavailable source, ambiguous attribution, or behavior that had no opportunity to occur.
- **Evidence strength:** High, Medium, or Low. It describes evidence quality, not model confidence.
- **Reported, not visible:** use when a self-report is not corroborated by the available trace. Do not say the person lacks the skill.
- **Example:** one or two concise, participant-readable evidence examples.
- **Next action:** one small action that can be attempted on a new task.

Avoid “failed,” “weak person,” “low performer,” “score,” “percentile,” and “confidence” in participant-facing rating language.

## Insufficient evidence

- Rating: “Not enough evidence”
- Change over time: “Not enough evidence to evaluate change over time.”
- Missing observation: “This was not visible in the records reviewed.”

## Required order

1. Private coaching report
2. What stands out
3. Five-area snapshot
4. Area details: four Ds only
5. How your approach changed: Adaptive Flexibility only
6. About this review
